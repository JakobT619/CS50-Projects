Spotify Wrapped is a feature presenting Spotify users’ 100 most played songs from the past year. In 2021, Spotify Wrapped calculated an “Audio Aura” for each user, a “reading of [their] two most 
prominent moods as dictated by [their] top songs and artists of the year.” Suppose Spotify determines an audio aura by looking at the average energy, valence, and danceability of a person’s top 100 songs
from the past year. In answers.txt, reflect on the following questions:

1. If songs.db contains the top 100 songs of one listener from 2018, how would you characterize their audio aura?
For these top 100 songs for a listener in 2018, I would characterize their audio auro as being largely
    purple and orange with a bit of blue. A lot of their music is high energy and something to get
    amped up to. There is also a handful of more emotional songs that represent the blueness of the
    listener.
    
2. Hypothesize about why the way you’ve calculated this aura might not be very representative of the listener. What better ways of calculating this aura would you propose?
It says that these auras are determined by calculating the average energy, valence, and danceability
    of a person's top 100 songs. A potentially better way to calculate aura would be to first find the
    range of energy, valence, and danceability values that correspond to each aura. Then,
    you could determine how many songs fall into each of these aura ranges. Then, the aura ranges with
    the most 'hits' or songs that match to that range would be the dominant aura. This method would
    more effectively manage outlier songs that could heavily sway an overall average based on only
    that one song being so polar.
